# Sylvan app-model generation audit — 2026-07-29

## Scope

This pass audits all **16 Sylvan records currently present in BoatBuilder**. It is a focused app-record audit, not a claim that every Sylvan model or production year has been cataloged.

## Result

- All 16 records now have an explicit researched disposition.
- Official annual tables were reconciled for the principal 2007–2012 walk-through models.
- Unresolved years remain specification-free and price-free.
- Seller shorthand and family names no longer carry blended specifications or values.

## High-impact corrections

| Record | Correction |
|---|---|
| Adventurer 1700 DC | Split into 2007, 2008, 2009–2010 and 2011–2012 factory generations. The tables document changes in depth, horsepower, side gauge, dry weight, persons and capacity. |
| Expedition Sport 1700 DC | Split at the 2009 horsepower/gauge change and the 2011 weight/capacity change. |
| Expedition Sport 1800 DC | Separated 2007–2008, 2009, the anomalous 2010 published table, and the 2011–2012 generation rather than blending contradictory dimensions. |
| Pro Sport 1600 DC | Grouped as one documented 2007–2010 factory generation. |
| Pro Sport 1700 / 1900 DC | Split 2007–2008 from 2009–2010 where horsepower or side gauge changes. |
| Sport Troller 1600 | Split the 2008 69-inch / 555-pound / 40-hp boat from the completely redesigned 2009–2010 81-inch / 700-pound / 60-hp hull. |
| Explorer 1600 DC | Split 2007–2010 from the 2011–2012 900-pound generation. |
| Super Sportster 16/17/18 OB | Removed the unsupported 1997 attribution. Their cited official source is the 1989 factory catalog, so only a 1989 catalog snapshot is retained. |
| Pro Select Dual 16/17 | Relabeled to the official 1996 archive catalog instead of presenting the source as an exact 1997 table. |
| Fish and Ski | Converted to an alias-only rejection. It is seller shorthand, not one factory model. |
| Viper | Converted to a family-umbrella rejection with no price or specifications; it also does not meet the walk-through-windshield requirement. |

Earlier Pro Fish, Pro Select, Super Sportster and Sport Troller transitions remain unresolved until additional annual factory tables can be visually reconciled.
