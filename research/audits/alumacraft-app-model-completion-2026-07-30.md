# Alumacraft existing-model strict completion audit

Date: 2026-07-30

## Scope

This audit covers only the 17 Alumacraft records already present in BoatBuilder. It does not add the manufacturer's complete historical product roster.

Completion rules:

1. No generation/evidence row may remain `unresolved`.
2. Known production spans for each existing record must be investigated.
3. Exact specifications remain attached only to the year or continuity range supported by factory evidence.
4. When complete factory tables could not be recovered, the interval closes as `source-exhausted-partial` or `model-identity-source-exhausted` and contains only the surviving facts.
5. Every physical/evidence row receives a used complete-package screening range.
6. Recent X-platform boats are separated from older boats carrying the same nominal model number.
7. Stable record IDs remain unchanged.

## Evidence examined

Primary sources:

- Alumacraft official catalog archive, covering annual catalogs from the 1980s through the current model years.
- Exact annual factory catalog tables already preserved in the repository for 1995, 2006–2012, 2014 and 2016.
- Official Alumacraft legacy/current model pages for the 2020–2026 Competitor, Trophy, Voyageur and Classic families.
- Official 2024 previous-model pages for the pre-X Competitor and Trophy platforms.

Supporting annual production evidence:

- J.D. Power annual Alumacraft model rosters. These were used to establish model identity, production continuity and published dry-weight changes when a complete factory table was unavailable.

Market screening evidence:

- Year/model valuation records and used-package market examples.
- Price ranges are complete-package screening guidance, not formal appraisals. Engine generation, hours, trailer, electronics, canvas, flooring and structural condition can move an individual package materially outside the listed range.

## Major corrections

### Dominator family

The previous audit treated the available factory snapshots as if they represented very short production runs.

- Dominator Sport 165 is now closed as 2006, 2007 and 2008–2013 evidence ranges.
- Dominator Sport 175 is now closed from 2006 through 2015.
- Dominator Sport 185 is no longer a one-year 2011 boat; annual records continue it through 2015.
- The old open-ended pre-2006 Dominator Sport placeholders were removed. Earlier 16-foot boats called simply Dominator were not silently relabeled as the later Sport models.

### Navigator family

- Navigator Sport 165 is represented from 2005 through 2013, with the exact 2010 factory table preserved separately.
- Navigator Sport 175 is represented from 2004 through 2013.
- The 2007 dimensional change remains explicit rather than blending the 2006 and later boats.

### Competitor family

- Competitor Sport 165 closes as a 2012–2019 model, with 2016 retained as the exact factory snapshot.
- The 17-foot-8-inch Competitor Sport/FSX 175 is separated from the 2025–2026 19-foot-2-inch X-platform redesign.
- The 18-foot-8-inch Competitor 185 is separated from the current 20-foot-2-inch X-platform redesign.
- Current factory marketing figures identified as boat/motor/trailer weights are not mislabeled as dry hull weights.

### Trophy family

- Trophy 170 now exposes documented late-1980s and 1990s weight/certification variations. Conflicting 1988–1989 evidence is withheld rather than averaged or guessed.
- Trophy 175 Sport now separates the 1998–2001 early Sport, the 2002–2006 1,215-pound identity, the 2007–2024 17-foot-8-inch pre-X hull, and the current 19-foot-2-inch X redesign.
- Trophy 185 now separates its early and intermediate identities, the 2007–2024 18-foot-8-inch pre-X hull, and the current 20-foot-2-inch X redesign.

### Other models

- Edge 175 and Edge 185 now cover their documented 2016–2021 runs rather than only the 2016 snapshot.
- Tournament Pro 185 now separates its 2002–2006, 2007–2010 and 2015–2021 identities, including the production gap.
- Voyageur 175 Sport closes as one documented 2014–2026 1,070-pound core generation.
- Classic 165 Sport is carried through the current 2026 model year.
