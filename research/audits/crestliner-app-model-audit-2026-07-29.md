# Crestliner app-model audit — 2026-07-29

## Scope

This batch audits the **15 Crestliner records already present in BoatBuilder**. It corrects source-year mismatches, separates documented same-name hulls, removes false model identities, and withholds specifications and prices where a model-year generation has not been established.

It does not claim to catalog every Crestliner model ever produced.

## Result

- **15 of 15** Crestliner app records have an explicit researched disposition.
- Every unresolved generation contains **no hull specifications and no pricing**.
- Fish Hawk 1700 is tied to the matching 2007–2008 factory hull and corrected to a **0.090-inch bottom**.
- Fish Hawk 1850 now separates the lighter 2007 boat from the heavier 2014 snapshot.
- Phantom Sportfish V160, V170 and V180 principal dimensions and weights were corrected; unverified horsepower and capacity values were removed.
- Sportfish 1750 no longer carries the false 1992 source identity.
- Sportfish 1850 separates the **18'2-inch / 1,470-pound** early boat from the **18'9-inch / 1,700-pound** 2017 boat.
- Super Hawk 1700 and 1800 aluminum gauges were corrected from 0.080-inch to **0.090-inch sides**.
- Vision 1600 and 1700 are correctly labeled as **2017** factory snapshots rather than 2015.
- Super Hawk 1600 is limited to its 2009 specification, including the corrected **1,295-pound capacity**.

## Evidence rule

Factory annual tables control when available. Secondary year-specific references are used only when the official archive cannot be read reliably, and those generations are labeled `secondary-verified`. Safety ratings are withheld when the source transcription is doubtful. Same-name records are not grouped unless the core length, beam, hull weight, capacity, horsepower, fuel, and construction data support it.
