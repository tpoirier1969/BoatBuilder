# Smoker Craft existing-model strict completion audit

Date: 2026-07-30

## Scope and completion result

This pass covers only the **18 Smoker Craft records already present in BoatBuilder**. It does not expand the app into a complete Smoker Craft model roster.

- Closed generation/evidence rows: **70**
- Remaining `unresolved` rows: **0**
- Physical/evidence rows with numeric used-package ranges: **68 of 68**
- Nonphysical listing aliases intentionally unpriced: **2** (`Millentia 182`, `Phaser`)
- Stable record IDs changed: **0**

A source-exhausted row is a closed research disposition. It reports only facts recoverable from factory catalogs, annual model rosters, exact-model fitment records and surviving year-specific references. It does not inherit a neighboring year's complete specification table.

## Important corrections

- **Fazer 172** closes at 1989–1995. The former 1996–1999 continuation was unsupported.
- **Fazer 178** is narrowed to 1998–1999.
- **Fazer 192** is represented across 1993–1999, with the exact 1995 factory snapshot isolated from source-exhausted surrounding years.
- **Ultima 175** is restored to its documented 1994–1999 production span rather than a single snapshot or later-name carryover.
- **Pro Angler 172** closes at 2004–2024. The current Pro Angler line no longer establishes a continuing 172.
- **Ultima 182** separates the early 2001–2002 boat, the 2015 reintroduction, the standard 2016–2018 hull, the distinct 2016 182SE variation, the later Legacy interval and the current Gen 2 boat.
- **Millentia 182** and **Phaser** remain alias-catcher rows, not invented boat models.

## Compact ideal-match markers

The asterisk is part of the existing one-line visible model text. It does not change the stable ID or add a new UI line.

- `*Millentia 172 WT`
- `*Pro Angler 172 (Primary; not Lund Pro Angler)`
- `*Pro Angler 172 XL`
- `*Ultima 172`

These were selected for a walk-through windshield configuration, useful beam and depth, credible Lake Superior capability, workable four-person space, appropriate flooring possibilities and at least some realistic used-market overlap with the project's price/towing targets. Larger or newer capable boats were not starred merely for being capable.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Smoker Craft annual factory catalogs and current factory specification pages.
2. Year-specific model rosters and exact-model fitment records for production boundaries.
3. Year-specific valuation references and observed used-package listings for screening-value calibration.

Principal sources include:

- Factory legacy catalog archive: https://www.smokercraft.com/resources/legacy-catalogs/
- Current factory model pages and 2025 brochure
- Exact-model fitment archive: https://rnr-marine.com/SmokerCraft.shtml
- Year-specific JD Power model and valuation records

Used ranges are screening values for complete packages. Motor technology and condition, transom/floor integrity, trailer, canvas, electronics and Great Lakes regional demand can move a listing outside the range.
