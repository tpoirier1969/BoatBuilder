# Princecraft existing-model strict completion audit

Date: 2026-07-31

## Scope

This pass covers only the **nine Princecraft records already present in BoatBuilder**. It does not add every Princecraft fishing boat or treat a successor model name as continuation of an older record.

Completion rules applied:

1. Remove every `unresolved` generation row.
2. Investigate the documented production span of each existing model identity.
3. Separate material changes in beam, hull weight, capacity certification, horsepower, plating, fuel capacity, or model identity.
4. Keep exact specifications inside the years supported by factory evidence.
5. Close incomplete surviving evidence explicitly rather than copy a neighboring year's full table.
6. Give every physical/evidence row a numeric used complete-package screening range.
7. Preserve all stable record IDs.

## Evidence hierarchy

Primary evidence:

- Princecraft product and catalog archive: https://www.princecraft.com/us/en/Dealers/Product-and-Catalog-Archives.aspx
- Princecraft annual archived product pages from 2011 through 2025
- Princecraft annual fishing-boat catalogs, especially 2010, 2012, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2023, 2024 and 2025
- Current and previous-model Princecraft pages used to identify successor-name boundaries

Supporting evidence:

- Contemporary specification records for the 2010–2011 Sport 172 where the full factory page was not recoverable
- Year-specific valuation and used-package references for screening-price calibration

Annual model presence establishes production identity. It does not authorize copying specifications from an adjacent year.

## High-impact corrections

### Holiday 162 WS

The former broad 2000s–2010s placeholder was unsupported. The factory archive establishes a usable lineage beginning with the 2012 Holiday DLX WS.

- **2012:** 84-inch, 834-lb hull with a 60-hp certification.
- **2013–2016:** same core dimensions and weight, but 75-hp certification.
- **2017–2019:** 0.087-inch bottom and 19-gallon tank replace the earlier 0.090-inch/20-gallon figures.
- **2020–2024:** wider 85-inch, 960-lb standard WS hull.
- The standard 75-hp model ends after 2024. The 2025–2026 lineup uses the distinct **Holiday 162 WS MAX** name and 60-hp package, so it is not silently appended to the standard record.

### Nanook 168 DLX WS

- Production closes at **2014–2023**.
- The 16-foot-6-inch, 88-inch, 1,044-lb core hull remains stable.
- **2017** is retained as a plating/fuel boundary: 0.090 inch and 20 gallons become 0.087 inch and 19 gallons.

### Platinum SE 176

- Production closes at **2014–2019**, not 2018.
- The 17-foot-6-inch, 92-inch, 1,435-lb hull remains stable.
- Fuel changes from 40 gallons to 37 gallons for **2018–2019**.
- The 176 disappears from the Platinum SE lineup for 2020 rather than inheriting Platinum SE 186 specifications.

### Pro 179 WS and Super Pro 176

- `Pro 179 WS` closes as the short **2009–2011** factory lineage.
- `Super Pro 176` closes at **2011–2015**.
- Neither record inherits later Sport, Platinum, Xperience or Kapture specifications.

### Sport 172

The record now exposes four documented hull/certification rows:

- **2010–2011:** early 92-inch, 1,225-lb boat.
- **2012–2016:** 91-inch, 1,255-lb, 125-hp hull.
- **2017–2022:** 91-inch, 1,377-lb, 125-hp hull.
- **2023:** same 1,377-lb core hull with a 150-hp and 1,572-lb certification.

The Sport 172 name ends after 2023. It is not merged with the 2024 Sport 175.

### Sport 187

- Production closes at **2014–2021**.
- The 18-foot-6-inch, 95-inch, 1,500-lb hull remains stable.
- Fuel changes from 40 gallons to 37 gallons for 2017.
- The record stops before the Sport 182 replacement in 2022 and does not inherit later Sport 185 or Sport 188 specifications.

### Sport 167 / Sport 164

- **2014–2016:** Sport 167 with 0.090-inch bottom and 20-gallon tank.
- **2017–2021:** renamed Sport 164 with 0.087-inch bottom and 19-gallon tank.
- The later-name production run continues through 2021, correcting the previous 2018 cutoff.

### Xpedition 170 WS

- The exact walk-through model closes as **2014–2016**.
- Later Xpedition 170 listings without the same WS identity do not inherit its exact dimensions or value assumptions.

## Value-method note

Prices are screening ranges for a complete running boat, ordinary period-appropriate outboard and usable trailer. They are not formal appraisals. Motor technology and condition, structural integrity, trailer condition, canvas, electronics, flooring and Great Lakes regional demand can move an individual package outside the range. The app's condition selector narrows the selected year/hull range; project boats require manual evaluation.
