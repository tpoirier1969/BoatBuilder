# Lund existing-model hidden-gap completion audit

Date: 2026-07-31

## Scope

This audit covers the 28 Lund records already present in BoatBuilder. It does not add every historical Lund model.

The prior data contained no rows literally labeled `unresolved`, but several records still compressed discontinuous model runs, materially different hulls, propulsion architectures, and windshield layouts into one apparent lineage. This pass applies the same strict completion standard used for the other completed manufacturers:

1. investigate the known production span of every existing record
2. separate material hull, certification, layout, or propulsion changes
3. preserve exact specifications only where the supporting record applies
4. close incomplete evidence intervals explicitly rather than borrowing adjacent specifications
5. provide numeric used complete-package screening ranges for every physical/evidence row
6. preserve all stable boat IDs

## Evidence hierarchy

Primary evidence:

- Lund factory catalog and brochure archive: https://www.lundboats.com/owner-resources.html
- Annual Lund factory catalogs, including 1975, 1997, 2003, 2005 and 2021–2026 material
- Current Lund model and configurator pages for Tyee, Alaskan and other active families
- Factory Red Book and model-year specification extracts already preserved in the repository

Supporting production evidence:

- Year-specific J.D. Power model rosters and published specification records
- Repository-preserved modern Lund generation extracts for 2012–2026
- Model-specific market and valuation records for package-price calibration

Annual rosters establish model identity and published changes. They do not authorize copying a complete specification table from another year.

## High-impact corrections

### Tyee lineages

- The old 18-foot Tyee outboard and I/O/ITS records remain separate. The I/O/ITS package is explicitly identified as a poor match for this project because of propulsion complexity and towing burden.
- `Tyee 1700` is no longer presented as an uninterrupted model. The surviving evidence supports a 1997 boat and a separate 2011–2012 return.
- `Tyee 1750` separates the 2007–2010 and 2011–2013 specification sets.
- The current `Tyee 1875` remains one documented 2021–2026 hull platform. Lund's published **1,950 lb** figure is maximum weight capacity, not dry hull weight. The standard boat weight remains **1,760 lb**. Price eras are separated without inventing a hull redesign.

### Alaskan family

- `Alaskan 1800 Sport` is represented as a discontinuous lineage: 2003, 2019–2020 and 2021–2023. Years in which the Sport identity was not established are not silently bridged.
- `Alaskan 1875 Sport` begins with the documented 2024 redesign and remains separate from the older 1800 record.
- `Alaskan 2000 Sport` also preserves its discontinuous 2003 and 2019–2023 evidence spans.

### Fisherman, Mr Pike and Pro-V

- `Fisherman 1800 OB` is split into 1997–1999, 2000–2002 and 2003–2006 published-weight generations.
- `Mr Pike 17` now exposes the 1999–2001, 2002 and 2003–2006 variations. Because the name appeared on different console/windshield layouts, the record tells the buyer to verify listing photographs rather than assuming a walk-through windshield.
- `Pro-V 1775` is separated into five evidence ranges from 1997 through 2016 and remains clearly identified as a non-walk-through candidate.
- `Pro-V 1800 SE` is limited to the verified 2003–2010 full-windshield SE lineage.

### Modern family transitions

- `Adventure Sport 1675` separates the 2020–2023 plywood boat from the 2024–2026 wood-free redesign.
- `Impact Sport 1775` separates the 2012–2017 and 2018–2021 hulls.
- `Crossover XS 1775` and `1875` expose the later redesigns and isolate the current 2026 platform rather than stretching one early specification across the entire run.
- `Rebel XL Sport 1650` preserves the production gap between the older Sport and the returned model.

## Compact ideal-match markers

## Value-method note

The values are screening ranges for a complete running boat, ordinary period-appropriate outboard and usable trailer. They are not formal appraisals. Motor technology and condition, trailer condition, structural integrity, canvas, electronics and regional Great Lakes demand can move a listing outside the range. The app's package-condition control narrows each selected year/hull range; project boats still require manual evaluation.
