# Alumacraft app-model audit batch 1 — 2026-07-28

## Scope

This batch covers the **17 Alumacraft records already present in BoatBuilder**. It does not claim every Alumacraft model or production year has been reconciled.

## Result

- 17 of 17 Alumacraft records have canonical generation metadata.
- Eleven broad records preserve one exact cited factory snapshot plus an unresolved-years option.
- Unresolved options contain no inherited specifications or pricing.
- Current X-generation Competitor and Trophy boats are blocked from inheriting older same-numbered hull data.
- The Magnum CS row is an explicit side-console family rejection, not a blended exact model.
- Trophy 185 Sport is corrected to the cited 2014 18'8", 98-inch-beam, 1,780-pound, 175-hp, 34-gallon basis.

## Remaining work

Dominator, Navigator, Trophy and early Competitor production still require year-by-year catalog reconciliation before unresolved spans can be replaced with verified generation boundaries.
