# Lund app-model audit — 2026-07-28

## Scope

This is a focused audit of the **28 Lund records present in BoatBuilder**. It does not claim that every Lund model ever built has been cataloged.

## Result

- 28 of 28 app records have explicit canonical generation metadata.
- Documented redesigns and materially different factory snapshots are separated.
- The older Tyee 1850 umbrella is split into distinct outboard and I/O / ITS records.
- The 2024 Alaskan 1875 redesign and current Tyee 1875 are separate records.
- Unresolved transition years do not inherit specifications or prices.
- Current similarly named boats are not merged into historical records.

## Important limitations

This completes the focused app-model safety pass, not a complete all-years Lund factory roster. Exact transition years remain unresolved for several long-running families, including parts of Alaskan, Explorer, Fisherman, Impact, Mr Pike, Pro-V and Tyee production.
