# Lund app-model audit — 2026-07-28

## Scope

This is a focused audit of the **28 Lund records already present in BoatBuilder**. It is not a claim that every Lund model ever built has been cataloged. No new off-scope Lund families were added.

## Result

- 28 of 28 app records have an explicit audit disposition.
- 8 records now expose more than one documented physical specification set.
- 28 records use only factory-exact, factory-generation, or corroborated secondary-verification statuses.
- Broad records were narrowed when the evidence did not support one specification across the advertised span.
- Current Impact XS, Fisherman 1875, Explorer 1700 and other similarly named but off-record boats were not merged into historical app records.
- Unknown transition years remain visibly unassigned rather than being invented.
