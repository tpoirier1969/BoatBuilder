# Tracker existing-model strict completion audit

Date: 2026-07-31

## Scope

This audit covers the eight Tracker records already present in BoatBuilder. It does not add Tracker's full historical product catalog.

Completion requirements used for this pass:

1. no generation/evidence row may remain `unresolved`
2. production spans must not bridge undocumented gaps or successor names
3. materially different published weights, dimensions, construction or certifications require separate choices
4. exact specifications remain only on the year/range supported by the cited record
5. source-exhausted rows contain only surviving facts and do not inherit adjacent specifications
6. every physical/evidence row receives a used complete-package screening range
7. stable boat IDs remain unchanged

## Evidence hierarchy

Primary/current evidence:

- Tracker official current and previous-model pages for Pro Guide V-175 WT, Targa V-18 WT and Targa V-19 WT
- year-specific Tracker factory pages preserved through the official previous-model archive

Supporting production and historical evidence:

- J.D. Power annual Tracker model rosters and year-specific specifications
- Boating World year-specific archived specification pages
- Boats.za.net year-specific model pages, used where the older factory page was no longer available
- model-specific fitment archives to confirm layout/name continuity

These supporting records establish annual identity and published specification changes. They do not authorize copying a complete specification table into a year for which it was not recovered.

## Important corrections

### Pro Guide V-175 WT

The previous record stored one exact 2020 snapshot and an open 2010–2029 placeholder. It now separates:

- 2010 early 17'3", 101-inch, 1,363-lb boat
- 2011 model-identity-only source-exhausted row
- 2012 16'11", 96-inch, 1,385-lb boat
- 2013–2017 97-inch, 1,385-lb boat
- 2018–2026 17'7", 97-inch, 1,525-lb platform

The current platform's factory average package weight is 2,985 lb, leaving useful room under the Maverick's 4,000-lb rating before normal gear, unlike the larger Targas.

Official current page:
https://www.trackerboats.com/deep-v/pro-guide-v-175-wt.html

### Targa V-18 WT

The Targa V-18 name covers a major weight progression rather than one stable decade-long hull:

- 2010–2011: 1,455 lb published weight
- 2012–2014: 1,697 lb, 37-gallon platform
- 2015–2016: 1,730 lb
- 2017–2019: 2,060 lb, .100-inch hull
- 2020: explicit .125-inch hull revision, 2,001 lb
- 2021–2026: roughly 2,205 lb current platform

The exact 2019 factory package was 3,704 lb before added gear. Later boats leave even less towing margin and remain secondary choices for this project.

## Value-method note

Prices are screening ranges for a complete running boat, an ordinary period-appropriate outboard and a usable trailer. The app's package-condition selector narrows the selected range. Engine technology and health, structural condition, trailer condition, electronics, canvas and regional demand can move an individual listing outside the range. A bad or untested motor, questionable floor/transom or major trailer problem remains a manual evaluation rather than a percentage discount.
