# Crestliner existing-model strict completion audit

Date: 2026-07-30

## Scope and completion result

This pass covers only the **15 Crestliner records already present in BoatBuilder**. It does not add every Crestliner model ever manufactured.

- Closed generation/evidence rows: **34**
- Remaining `unresolved` rows: **0**
- Physical/evidence rows with numeric used-package ranges: **34 of 34**
- Stable record IDs changed: **0**

Exact annual factory tables are retained when available. Where production identity survives but a complete compatible table does not, the interval is closed as source-exhausted and does not inherit specifications from a neighboring year.

## Important corrections

- **Fish Hawk 1700** closes as a documented 2007–2010 generation rather than an exact snapshot plus an open tail.
- **Fish Hawk 1750** now covers the documented 2002–2026 model line across early, 2007–2010, 2011–2013, heavier 2014–2017, pre-AP-X and current AP-X intervals.
- **Fish Hawk 1850** separates the early 2004–2013 boat, the heavier 2014–2023 generation and the current AP-X generation.
- **Sportfish 1750** is limited to the 2000–2005 outboard model. It no longer mixes in the materially different 1990s V175 Sportfish sterndrive.
- **Sportfish 1850** separates the early 18-foot-2-inch line, the later 18-foot-9-inch generation and the current AP-X boat.
- **Super Hawk 1800** ends in 2012, before the 1850 replacement.
- **Vision 1600** closes at 2014–2018 and **Vision 1700** at 2015–2018.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Crestliner annual factory catalogs and current factory specification pages.
2. Year-specific model rosters and exact-model fitment records for production boundaries.
3. Year-specific valuation references and observed used-package listings for screening values.

Principal sources include:

- Crestliner factory catalog archive: https://www.crestliner.com/documents.html
- Current Fish Hawk and Sportfish factory pages
- Exact-model fitment archive: https://rnr-marine.com/Crestliner.shtml
- Year-specific JD Power model and valuation records

Used ranges are package-screening estimates rather than formal appraisals. Exact motor, trailer, structural condition, canvas, electronics and regional demand remain decisive.
