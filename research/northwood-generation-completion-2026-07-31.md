# Northwood existing-model generation completion — 2026-07-31

## Scope

Focused completion of the single Northwood record already present in BoatBuilder: `170 Pro Tourney`. This is not a complete factory-roster expansion. The stable boat ID is preserved.

## Chronology and identity

- A surviving exact 1994 listing establishes that sellers used `170 Pro Tourney` before the strongest configuration-fitment span. No year-specific factory specification table survived in the evidence set, so 1994 is retained as a source-exhausted evidence row.
- ShoreTex custom-cover fitment records list `170 Pro Tourney SC` and `170 Pro Tourney DC` for 1995–1997. Carver bimini fitment records instead call them `171 Pro Tourney SC` and `172 Pro Tourney DC`. Both sources verify side-console and dual-console/windshield configurations, but their nomenclature conflict is disclosed rather than normalized into invented separate hulls.
- A surviving 1998 owner listing calls the boat a `170 Pro Tourney`, carries a 90 hp Mariner and reports an owner-measured 17 ft 8 in length. Because ShoreTex ends the formal 170 SC/DC span in 1997 and introduces the 175 Supreme DC in 1998, the seller-labeled 1998 boat remains a separate source-exhausted evidence row.

## Pricing evidence

- A May 2024 HiBid auction realized $3,375 for a running 1997 package with 75 hp Force, trailer, console/windshield, trolling motor and electronics; the trim-and-tilt cylinder leaked.
- Other exact-family evidence includes a $2,200-era 1996 auction result, a $5,500 1997 asking example, and late-1990s complete-package listings with 90–100 hp motors.
- The app therefore uses screening ranges of $2,500–$4,500 for 1994, $2,800–$5,500 for 1995–1997, and $3,000–$6,000 for the seller-labeled 1998 row. These are complete-package ranges, not bare-hull values.

## Suitability decision

Northwood remains `Secondary` and receives no compact ideal-match star. A verified dual-console/windshield option is useful, but the documented SC beam is only 83 inches, carpeting is typical, factory dry weight/deadrise/capacity remain unrecovered, and surviving owner reports describe light hulls that blow around readily. It may be usable in carefully chosen Lake Superior conditions, but it is not an ideal match for Tod and Donna's search.
