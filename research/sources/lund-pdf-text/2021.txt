Quality Features
What sets Lund apart from other aluminum boat builders is
our ability to get more out of the material. We build our boats
to a different standard, with longitudinal stringers, double-
riveted seams and sophisticated twin-plating that doesn’t add
excessive weight.


       • Aluminum corner castings
       • Composite & Aluminum Transoms
       • Durable wraparound windshield
       • Easy-access service portals
       • Flushmount horn
       • Heavy-duty consoles
       • Heavy-duty transom and gunwales
       • Lighted instrumentation, including fuel
         gauge, trim gauge, speedometer,
         tachometer and voltmeter
       • Long-lasting oven-cured finish
       • Treated marine grade plywood
       • Molded interior surfaces and side panels
       • Navigational lighting
       • No-pinch integrated seat hinges
       • Recessed stainless-steel cleats
       • Stainless steel bow rails
       • Vinyl wrapped interior
       • Wider chine for smoother, more stable ride
