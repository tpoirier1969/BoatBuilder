     the Lund Difference
     Since 1948, Lund has been delivering boats of legendary
     quality with industry-leading innovation and versatility from
     the heart of lake country in New York Mills, Minnesota.
     Constructed with professional grade materials to withstand a lifetime of use, Lund boats are prized
     by resorts, fishing guides and professionals. Our boats are carefully designed with features and
     layouts to meet the needs of both hardcore anglers and fishing families. For nearly eight decades,
     Lunds have been crafted by passionate enthusiasts who don’t just build our boats—they own them.
