# BoatBuilder existing-model generation completion status — 2026-07-29

## Controlling scope

BoatBuilder is refining the models already present in the app. It is **not** conducting a complete roster audit of every model offered by every manufacturer.

An existing manufacturer set is considered complete only when:

1. no `unresolved` generation rows remain
2. all available year-specific records for the existing models have been checked
3. materially different specifications are represented as separate generations or annual variations
4. source-exhausted gaps are closed explicitly without borrowing nearby specifications
5. every physical generation has a defensible used complete-package value range
6. aliases, seller shorthand and family umbrellas are closed as unpriced non-model dispositions

## Current canonical state

- Canonical boat records: **170**
- Every boat record has canonical `designGenerations` metadata.
- Unresolved generations remain specification-free and price-free while they are being researched.
- **Sylvan is the first manufacturer set completed under the clarified standard:** 16 existing records, 39 physical generations/variations, zero unresolved rows, and a used-value range on every physical variation.
- Earlier detailed passes remain useful safety work, but they are not called complete until their open generations and missing generation-specific values are finished.

## Preserved detailed work

- **Lund:** major Tyee, Alaskan, Pro-V and other same-name substitutions were separated. No unresolved rows remain, but ten physical variations still lack defensible generation-specific used values.
- **Alumacraft:** Dominator 165/175/185 and Navigator 175 are reconciled against 2006-2012 factory catalogs, including the 2011 Dominator 185 introduction and the 2012 change to Competitor. Ten open rows remain elsewhere.
- **Crestliner:** false identities and several major same-name substitutions are corrected. Twelve open rows remain.
- **Smoker Craft:** aliases and unsupported exact-year claims are narrowed and several redesigns are split. Fourteen open rows remain.
- **Starcraft / Starweld:** documented 2014-2015 changes are separated and unsafe cross-decade pricing is removed. Seventeen open rows remain across the two brands.
- **Sylvan:** all existing-model spans are now closed and every physical variation has used complete-package guidance.
