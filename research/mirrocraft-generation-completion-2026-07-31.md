# MirroCraft generation completion — 2026-07-31

## Scope and stopping rule

This audit covers only the seven MirroCraft records already present in BoatBuilder. It does not add a complete MirroCraft product roster. Every existing record was followed through the available annual model rosters and surviving factory sheets until the open span could be converted to a physical generation, a documented partial-evidence generation, or a nonphysical family-level rejection.

Result: 16 closed generation/evidence rows (15 physical generations plus one family rejection), zero MirroCraft `unresolved` rows, and generation-contained complete-package screening ranges for every physical generation. The generic Troller family catcher is intentionally unpriced because it is not one physical model.

## Market calibration

Ranges are screening values for complete used packages—boat, age-appropriate outboard and usable trailer—not bare-hull book values. The app's condition selection narrows the range after the year/hull choice.

Useful live anchors at the time of research included:

- 2026 Aggressor Pro MX 1773 WT with 115 hp and trailer offered at $37,500: https://resortersmarineoutdoors.com/products/2026-mirrocraft-aggressor-pro-mx-1773-wt
- 2026 Holiday 1768 WT offered at $35,800: https://resortersmarineoutdoors.com/pages/mirrocraft-boats
- 2026 Holiday 168 WT offered at $28,300: https://resortersmarineoutdoors.com/pages/mirrocraft-boats
- Dealer inventory showing 2017 F1766 at $25,250, current F176 at $40,977 and current F1768 at $40,749: https://www.soidrvmarine.com/search/inventory/brand/MirroCraft
- Current 2025 Dual Impact 176/115 hp package at $38,995: https://www.triple-r-sales.com/mirrocraft-boats-for-sale-c2545907

Older eras use broader ranges because motor age, repower status, floor/transom condition, electronics and trailer condition dominate surviving package prices. A project/unknown package remains a manual evaluation rather than a mechanical percentage discount.

## Ideal-match markers

Three records receive the compact leading star:

- `*Aggressor Pro MX 1773 WT`
- `*Dual Impact 17' family / 176`
- `*Holiday 17' family / 1768`

The star identifies records containing a realistic wide-beam, deep, factory walk-through, vinyl-floor-capable generation with a plausible older used-package route toward the target. It does not assert that every year or every package inside the family meets the towing or price target.

## Known evidence limitations

- Published beam entries for the 1995–2002 F1745 conflict. The app reports that conflict and tells the user to verify the capacity plate; it does not invent a redesign.
- Some 2000s rosters preserve model identity and weight more reliably than complete construction tables. Those generations use `source-exhausted-partial` and show only the recovered facts.
- Asking prices are not sale prices. They are used as market anchors and are widened for engine, trailer and condition uncertainty.
